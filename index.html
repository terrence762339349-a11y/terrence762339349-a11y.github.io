About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (Shenzhen), with a PhD in Land Resource Management from Renmin University of China. My research focuses on spatiotemporal data analytics, GIS and remote sensing, land use and transportation interaction, and urban green and low-carbon development.

I have participated in national-level research projects, including the National Key R&D Program of China (2022YFB3903700), and have published peer-reviewed articles in journals such as Land and Land Degradation & Development.
