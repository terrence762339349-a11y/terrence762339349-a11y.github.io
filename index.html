About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at The Chinese University of Hong Kong. My research focuses on spatiotemporal data intelligence, land use change, urban sustainability, and transportation–land interactions.

Research Interests

  • Spatiotemporal data modeling and analysis
  • Land use and land cover change
  • Urban sustainability and low-carbon development
  • Transport–land use interaction
  • GIS, Remote Sensing, and Urban Analytics

Publications (Selected)

  • He, Y., Liu, X. R., Wu, D. F., et al. (2025). Ecological Risks and Patterns Associated with Land Use/Cover Changes. Land Degradation & Development.
  • He, Y., et al. (2024). Balancing Economic Growth and Environmental Conservation. Scientific Reports.
