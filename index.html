About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (Shenzhen), holding a PhD in Land Resource Management from Renmin University of China. My research interests focus on spatiotemporal data analytics, GIS and remote sensing, land use and transportation interaction, and urban green and low-carbon development.

I have participated in several national-level research projects, including the National Key R&D Program of China (2022YFB3903700). My work has been published in peer-reviewed journals such as Land, Land Degradation & Development, and Scientific Reports.
