Research Overview

My research focuses on spatiotemporal data analytics and intelligent modeling, with applications in GIS and remote sensing, land use and transportation interaction, and urban green and low-carbon development. I aim to integrate geospatial big data, machine learning, and planning theory to support evidence-based urban and regional sustainability policies.

Research Themes

My research explores the mechanisms, patterns, and policy implications of urban and regional systems through the integration of spatial data science, geospatial technologies, and planning-oriented analytical frameworks.

Spatiotemporal Data Analytics and Intelligent Modeling

Development of high-reliability spatiotemporal data processing methods and intelligent models for complex urban systems analysis.

GIS and Remote Sensing Applications

Multi-source remote sensing data fusion for land use/cover change detection, ecological risk assessment, and environmental monitoring.

Land Use and Transportation Interaction

Quantitative analysis of coupling mechanisms between transportation infrastructure development and land use evolution.

Urban Sustainability and Low-Carbon Development

Integrated assessment of green and low-carbon development in megacities, including carbon source–sink accounting and socio-natural system coupling.
