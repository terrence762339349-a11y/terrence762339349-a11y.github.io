Research Overview

My research focuses on spatiotemporal data analytics and intelligent modeling, with applications in GIS and remote sensing, land use and transportation interaction, and urban green and low-carbon development.

Research Themes

Spatiotemporal Data Analytics and Intelligent Modeling

High-reliability spatiotemporal data processing and intelligent modeling for complex urban systems.

GIS and Remote Sensing Applications

Multi-source remote sensing data fusion for land use change detection and ecological risk assessment.

Land Use and Transportation Interaction

Coupling mechanisms between transportation infrastructure and land use evolution.

Urban Sustainability and Low-Carbon Development

Integrated assessment of green and low-carbon development in megacities.
