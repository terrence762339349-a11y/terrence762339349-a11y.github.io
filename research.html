Research Overview

My research focuses on urban and regional systems through spatial data science, geospatial technologies, and planning-oriented analytical frameworks, with particular attention to sustainability and low-carbon transitions.

Explore Research

Research Themes

Spatiotemporal Data Analytics

Spatiotemporal modeling and big data analytics to reveal dynamic urban processes and regional evolution patterns.

GIS and Remote Sensing

GIS-based spatial analysis and remote sensing techniques for land use, environmental monitoring, and urban dynamics assessment.

Land–Transportation Interaction

Integrated analysis of land use and transportation systems, focusing on spatial coupling mechanisms and planning implications.

Urban Sustainability & Low-Carbon Development

Assessment frameworks and policy evaluation for green, low-carbon, and sustainable urban development pathways.
